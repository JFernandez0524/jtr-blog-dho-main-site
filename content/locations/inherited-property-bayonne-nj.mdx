## Probate for a Bayonne Estate (Hudson County Surrogate's Court)

Bayonne estates go through the **Hudson County Surrogate's Court**, located at **595 Newark Avenue, Jersey City, NJ 07306** (Hudson County Administration Building complex). 

While probate runs its course:
- The property continues to accrue carrying costs — property taxes, insurance premiums, utilities, and water bills.
- If the inherited house is a multi-family with existing tenants, the estate inherits the landlord responsibility under New Jersey's Anti-Eviction Act (N.J.S.A. 2A:18-61.1).
- Vacant property insurance endorsements are required if the home sits empty for more than 30–60 days.

Before making any quick decisions or accepting a cash offer, establish where the estate stands legally and get an honest, empirical valuation of what the building will yield on the open market.
